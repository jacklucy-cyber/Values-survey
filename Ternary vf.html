A ternary plot is a triangular graph that shows the proportions of three variables that add up to a fixed total.

In this survey, you will be asked to click the point on the graph that you believe best represents the balance between three values in the present day: Efficiency, Personal Autonomy, and Human Connection.

You will then be presented with a scenario for a future set in the year 2060 and asked to click the point on the graph that you believe best represents the balance between the three values in this future scenario.
